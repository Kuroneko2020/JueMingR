Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: one finished Windows application icon for the Chinese project 决明 (JueMing). Its identity is clarity and light. It is NOT branded around the Latin letter R.
Primary request: design a simple original geometric sun-and-moon emblem, inspired by the two complementary ideas 日 (sun) and 月 (moon) that together suggest 明 (brightness), without drawing any written characters.
Composition: a single cohesive, large centered emblem consisting of a warm-gold circular sun on the left and a mint-teal crescent or open circular arc on the right. Both shapes use generous, visually balanced geometric proportions. Their adjacent silhouettes form a unified calm mark with a clean gap and clear contrast. Keep the shapes simple enough to read as two complementary celestial forms at 16px and 32px.
Backdrop: a deep indigo rounded square app tile occupying about 90 percent of a square canvas. Outside the rounded square is genuine transparent alpha. Use ample but controlled padding.
Style: refined flat geometric identity, confident simple silhouettes, crisp edges, balanced spacing. Flat warm muted gold, jade/mint teal and dark indigo. No gradients, texture, shadow, outlines or tiny strokes.
Constraints: absolutely no R, no Latin letters, no Chinese characters, no typography, no words, no four-point sparkle, no stars, no ray bursts, no chat bubbles, no AI assistant icon, no installer arrows, no shield, no plant or tree, no Terraria icon imitation. One icon only, not a contact sheet, not a mockup, not a scene. The sun and crescent are the whole mark.
